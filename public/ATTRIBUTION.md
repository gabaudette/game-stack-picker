# Logo attribution

Tool names and logos belong to their respective owners. Logos identify the tools; no endorsement is implied. Brand guidelines and trademark rights remain applicable.

Most logos are sourced from [SVGL](https://svgl.app/), whose collection is distributed under the [MIT license](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/LICENSE). Official vendor SVGs are used where available. Vendor SVG files retain their embedded notices. GameMaker, GDevelop, and itch.io monochrome SVGs use a light fill for contrast.

Godot-related entries use the Godot icon from its official press kit; engine-specific entries may share their engine logo. Visual events and GML use their host-engine marks. Tools without an available SVG use a text monogram.
